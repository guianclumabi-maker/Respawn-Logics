# Respawn Logics — Beta Program Agreement

Welcome to the Respawn Logics Beta Program. By participating, you get early access to a next-generation HR and Payroll platform designed specifically for the Philippines. Your feedback will directly shape the product.

Because this is a beta release, there are known functional limitations that you should be aware of before onboarding your real company data. These are not bugs, but rather features slated for upcoming releases.

## Known Limitations

Please review the following limitations:

### Payroll
- **Tax Annualization**: Currently requires manual calculation at year-end.
- **Premium-pay Overlaps**: Edge cases involving multiple premium pay types on the same day may result in underpayment; please verify timesheets on complex holidays.
- **Loan Balances**: Outstanding loan balances are not auto-tracked and deducted automatically yet.
- **Unworked-Holiday Eligibility**: Eligibility for regular holiday pay on unworked days (per Art. 94 of the Labor Code) is not auto-determined by the system.

### Reporting
- **Government Reports**: Automated e-file formats for government portals (e.g., SSS R-3 DAT, BIR Alphalist) are not yet generated by the system.

### AI Companion
- **Chat History**: Previous chat sessions do not reload.
- **Media**: File attachments and voice inputs are not supported in this beta version.

## Operational Procedures

During the beta program, we strongly recommend running the payroll engine in **parallel** with your existing payroll process for at least one full cycle (see the Payroll Parallel-Run Playbook) before fully transitioning.

*By proceeding with onboarding, you acknowledge these limitations.*
