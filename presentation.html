01 · Market Context & Problem

The Fragmented HR & Payroll Crisis

Managing growing organizations with scattered spreadsheets and legacy tools creates severe compliance risks, operational delays, and friction.

⚠️

Statutory Compliance Maze

Frequent changes in Philippine statutory rules (SSS brackets, WISP tiering, PhilHealth 5% rate, BIR graduated tax) lead to frequent miscalculations and heavy BIR/DOLE penalties.

🧩

Tool & Data Fragmentation

Candidate resumes sit in emails, attendance in biometric punch files, payroll in fragile Excel spreadsheets, and employee grievance logs in separate tickets. Zero single source of truth.

📉

Poor Employee Trust

Lack of transparent mobile payslips, confusing deduction breakdowns, and slow manual leave approval cycles degrade employee retention and HR team productivity.
